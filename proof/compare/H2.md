# Gem Vault: three repairs and three additions

The folder `game/` holds a working game. [`SPEC.md`](SPEC.md) beside this file is the text
it was built from; read it first. Nobody will answer questions.

## Repairs

A player sent this report. Find the cause of each and repair it. What `SPEC.md` says is
what is right.

1. "In the first level I left one gem and the door let me through anyway."
2. "In the second level one of the guards walks straight through a wall."
3. "In the third level a guard caught me and I was back in the second level."

## Additions

4. **Lives.** The player has three lives, shown on screen in every level as `Lives 3`,
   `Lives 2`, `Lives 1`. Touching a guard takes one life and starts the level again as
   before. With no life left a screen shows `Game over`; pressing any arrow key there
   starts level 1 again with three lives. Lives carry over from level to level.
5. **A key and a gate in level 2.** Level 2 gets one key and one gate. The gate stands
   in the only way to at least one gem and blocks the player like a wall. Walking onto the
   key collects it and opens the gate. When the level starts again, the key is back and
   the gate is closed.
6. **Level 4.** After level 3 comes a new level, named `Level 4` on screen, and the end
   screen comes after it. It is at least 13 by 9 cells and holds at least 4 gems, 2 guards,
   2 crates, and one key with its gate. It can be won.

## What "done" means

- Every file of the game is still inside `game/`, and the game starts in the same way as
  before, with no error and no warning.
- The three reports no longer happen, and nothing else `SPEC.md` asks for has stopped
  working.
- The game's own tests pass. Tests that no longer describe the game are changed to
  describe it; each of 1 to 6 has a test of its own.

When done, say for each of 1 to 6 what was wrong or what was added, and in which files.

## Screen pass checks

A person plays the game as the agent left it with the four arrow keys only, started in the
engine's ordinary way. Each check is "do this, see that". The fifteen checks of
[`GEMVAULT-CHECKS.md`](GEMVAULT-CHECKS.md) apply first, with check 15 read as "win level 3
and see `Level 4`"; then:

| # | Do | See |
|---|---|---|
| 16 | (repair 1) In level 1 leave exactly one gem and walk into the door. | The door holds. |
| 17 | (repair 2) Watch every guard of level 2 for twenty seconds. | No guard enters or passes a wall cell. |
| 18 | (repair 3) In level 3 walk into a guard. | Level 3 starts again, not level 2. |
| 19 | Start the game. | `Lives 3` on screen in level 1. |
| 20 | In level 2 walk into a guard. | `Lives 2`; the level starts again. |
| 21 | Reach level 3 after losing one life. | Level 3 shows `Lives 2`. |
| 22 | Lose the last life. | `Game over`. An arrow key starts level 1 with `Lives 3`. |
| 23 | In level 2, before taking the key, walk into the gate for two seconds. | The gate holds, and at least one gem lies behind it with no other way round. |
| 24 | Take the key. | The key disappears and the gate opens or is gone; the gem behind it can be collected. |
| 25 | Take the key, then walk into a guard. | The level starts again with the key back and the gate closed. |
| 26 | Win level 3. | `Level 4`: at least 13 by 9 cells, 4 gems, 2 guards, 2 crates, a key and a gate. |
| 27 | Win level 4. | The end screen with `You win`. |

A game passes when all twenty-seven hold. A level a person cannot win in ten tries fails
its check and the checks after it.
