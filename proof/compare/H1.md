# Held-out spec 1: a gravity-flip room puzzle (2D)

Written 2026-11-27. **Not run on any engine, and not to be run before the engine commit is
pinned.** The text between the two lines below is what an agent is given, unchanged, as
`SPEC.md`. Everything outside them is for the people running the comparison.

- **Source.** The 20 Games Challenge, page `content/en/games/vvvvvv.md` at commit
  `ac7eeae150` of its repository (MIT, © 2022 Matthew S). The name, characters, art and
  music of the game that page describes are not used; the spec says "the player" and
  "a room".
- **Why this one.** Chosen by the owner (2026-11-27) from three pages of that challenge.
  It is a public task written by people with no interest in either engine, and it is 2D,
  the size Lite is for.
- **What was added to the page** (marked *(added)* below). Confirmed by the owner,
  2026-11-27: spikes, a moving enemy, a moving platform and a disappearing platform are all
  four required; the page says a room "can contain" them. Chosen by the author of this file
  and not put to the owner one by one (the owner's instruction of that day was to go on
  without asking): at least four rooms and the two kinds of exit, riding a moving platform
  on its underside, the disappearing platform coming back after a death, the end point
  with its words, the keys, what a checkpoint keeps, and that each of the four obstacles
  lies on the way.
- **Sound.** The page asks for sound effects and music. An agent in this comparison cannot
  make or copy a sound file, so both engines are given the same five, already inside
  `game/sounds/`: the files of [`h1-sounds/`](h1-sounds), made from arithmetic by
  [`scripts/h1sounds.ts`](../../scripts/h1sounds.ts) (8-bit mono WAV at 8 kHz, no outside
  source). `game/` holds nothing else at the start.
- **Not checked against Kiln.** Whether Kiln can build every line was deliberately not
  tried: a held-out spec that was fitted to the engine would be a development spec. What
  is known from reading alone, written down before any run: Kiln has one gravity for the
  whole world, lands and rides only downwards, has no camera that goes room by room, and
  no component for any of this; a script would have to do it.

# Gravity flip

A written spec for one small 2D game. Build it from this text alone. Nobody will answer
questions.

## The game

1. The game is in 2D, seen from the side. The player walks left and right with the left
   and right arrow keys. There is no jump.
2. Gravity pulls the player either down or up. Pressing Space flips it, but only while
   the player is standing on something: on a floor when gravity is down, on a ceiling when
   it is up. In the air the key does nothing. After a flip the player falls to the other
   side and stands there, drawn upside down. *(added: the key, and the drawing)*
3. A player who walks off the end of a platform falls the way gravity points at that
   moment.
4. The world is at least four rooms. A room fills the screen, and one room is shown at a
   time. Leaving a room through an opening at its edge shows the room on that side, with
   the player coming in at the matching place; going back the same way shows the first
   room again. At least one pair of rooms is joined side by side and at least one pair is
   joined one above the other. *(added: the number of rooms and both kinds of joint)*
5. Spikes kill the player on touch. A moving enemy, which goes back and forth along a
   fixed straight path forever, kills the player on touch.
6. There are checkpoints, at least two, in different rooms. A checkpoint lights up when
   the player touches it, and the one touched last is the only one lit. A player who dies
   comes back at the last checkpoint touched, in that checkpoint's room and with gravity
   as it was when the checkpoint was touched, also when the death was in another room.
   Before any checkpoint is touched, the player comes back where the game began.
7. There is a moving platform that goes back and forth forever. It carries a player who
   stands on it: on its top when gravity is down, and on its underside when gravity is up.
   *(added: the underside)*
8. There is a disappearing platform. About one second after the player first stands on
   it, it is gone, and what stood on it falls. It is back when the player dies. *(added:
   that it comes back)*
9. Each of the four (spikes, enemy, moving platform, disappearing platform) lies on the way
   from the start to the end: the spikes and the enemy have to be passed, the moving
   platform has to be ridden, and the disappearing platform has to be crossed. *(added)*
10. The game has sound, from the five files in `game/sounds/`, used as they are:
    `flip.wav` when gravity flips, `death.wav` when the player dies, `checkpoint.wav` when
    a checkpoint lights up, `win.wav` at the end, and `music.wav` playing again and again
    for the whole game.
11. One room holds the end point, which can be told apart from everything else. Touching
    it shows the words `You win`, and the game is over. *(added)*
12. The game can be finished: there is a way from the start to the end point for a person
    playing with these three keys.

## What "done" means

- Every file of the game is inside the folder `game/`.
- The game starts in the engine's ordinary way of running a game, with no error and no
  warning.
- Everything under "The game" holds for a person who plays it with the left and right
  arrow keys and Space. Check as much of that as the engine lets you check without a
  person.

When done, list the files and say how the game is started.

## Screen pass checks

A person plays the game as the agent left it, with the left and right arrow keys and Space
only, sound on, started the way the agent's last message says. Each check is "do this, see
that".

| # | Do | See and hear |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Start the game. | A room seen from the side with the player standing on a floor. No error or warning where the game was started. Music is heard, and starts again when it ends. |
| 2 | Hold left, then right. | The player walks each way and is stopped by walls. No key makes the player jump. |
| 3 | Standing on a floor, press Space once. | The flip sound. The player rises to the ceiling and stands on it upside down. |
| 4 | Press Space again and, while the player is in the air, press it several more times. | Only the first press counts: the player lands on the floor, and no flip sound is heard for the presses in the air. |
| 5 | Walk off the end of a platform, once with gravity down and once with gravity up. | The player falls down the first time and up the second. |
| 6 | Leave the first room through an opening. | Another room fills the screen; the player comes in at the matching edge. |
| 7 | Go back the same way. | The first room again, as it was. |
| 8 | Visit every room. | At least four. At least one is entered sideways and at least one from above or below. |
| 9 | Touch spikes. | The death sound. The player comes back where the game began (no checkpoint touched yet). |
| 10 | Touch a checkpoint. | It lights up, and the checkpoint sound is heard. |
| 11 | Touch a second checkpoint in another room. | It lights up; the first one is no longer lit when seen again. |
| 12 | Go to a room other than that checkpoint's and die there. | The player comes back at the second checkpoint, in its room. |
| 13 | Watch the moving enemy for twenty seconds, then touch it. | It goes back and forth on a straight path. Touching it kills as in check 9, with the player back at the last checkpoint. |
| 14 | Stand on the moving platform with gravity down and press no key. | The player is carried along with it. |
| 15 | Stand on the underside of a moving platform with gravity up and press no key. | The player is carried along with it. |
| 16 | Stand on the disappearing platform and press no key. | After about one second it is gone and the player falls. |
| 17 | Die, and look at the disappearing platform again. | It is back. |
| 18 | Look for a way from the start to the end point that passes no spikes and no enemy, rides no moving platform and crosses no disappearing platform. | There is none: each of the four is on the way. |
| 19 | Reach the end point. | It could be told apart before it was touched. The win sound, and the words `You win`. |

A game passes when all nineteen hold. A game a person cannot finish in ten tries from the
last checkpoint before any one obstacle fails check 19 and every check that could not be
reached.
