# Lap race

A written spec for one small 3D game. Build it from this text alone. Nobody will answer
questions.

## The game

1. The game is in 3D. The player drives a small car around a closed track. The camera
   sits behind and above the car, looks where the car points, and follows it.
2. Up accelerates, Down brakes and then reverses, Left and Right steer. The car only
   turns while it is moving. With no key held it slows to a stop within a few seconds.
   It has a top speed.
3. The track is a loop of road with at least four corners, at least one to the left and
   one to the right. Walls stand along both sides. Hitting a wall slows the car and turns
   it aside; the car never passes through a wall and never gets stuck in one for good.
4. Off the road, between road and wall, the ground is rough: the car's top speed there is
   at most half of what it is on the road.
5. One stretch of the track has a ramp followed by a gap in the road. Gravity acts on the
   car. Taken at speed the car flies over the gap and lands on the far side; taken too
   slowly it drops into the gap. A car in the gap is put back on the road just before the
   ramp, standing still, after about one second.
6. At least six loose cones stand on the road. They are not walls: a car that hits one
   knocks it away, it tumbles and slides, and it stays where it comes to rest until the
   race is started again.
7. A race is three laps. A lap counts only when the car has passed every part of the loop
   in order; crossing the line backwards, or turning round after the start and crossing
   it again, does not count.
8. One other car, driven by the computer, races too. It follows the track, takes the
   ramp, and finishes three laps by itself in a time a careful player can beat. The two
   cars cannot pass through each other.
9. A race opens with a countdown `3`, `2`, `1`, `Go`. Neither car moves before `Go`.
10. During the race the screen shows the lap as `Lap 1/3`, the time of the race in
    seconds with tenths, the player's place as `1st` or `2nd`, and the car's speed.
11. When the player finishes the third lap a panel shows `Finished`, the place, the total
    time, and the time of each of the three laps. Enter goes back to the title.
12. The game opens on a title screen with the game's name and `Press Enter`. Enter starts
    a race.

## What "done" means

- Every file of the game is inside the folder `game/`.
- The game starts in the engine's ordinary way of running a game, with no error and no
  warning.
- Everything under "The game" holds for a person who plays with the arrow keys and Enter.
  Check as much of that as the engine lets you check without a person.

When done, list the files and say how the game is started.

## Screen pass checks

| # | Do | See |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Start the game. | A title and `Press Enter`. No error or warning where it was started. |
| 2 | Press Enter; hold Up during the countdown. | `3`, `2`, `1`, `Go` in 3D behind a car; no car moves before `Go`. |
| 3 | Hold Up after `Go`. | The car speeds up to a top speed; the camera follows; `Lap 1/3`, a running time, a place and a speed are shown. |
| 4 | Release every key. | The car slows and stops within a few seconds. |
| 5 | From rest press Left; then drive and steer both ways. | At rest the car does not turn. Moving, it turns each way and the camera turns with it. |
| 6 | Hold Down from rest. | The car goes backwards. |
| 7 | Drive into a wall at speed. | The car is slowed and deflected, stays on the track side of the wall, and can drive on. |
| 8 | Drive on the ground beside the road. | Top speed visibly lower than on the road, about half or less. |
| 9 | Hit a cone. | It is knocked away, tumbles or slides, and is still where it stopped a lap later. |
| 10 | Take the ramp at top speed. | The car leaves the ground, clears the gap and lands on the road. |
| 11 | Take the ramp slowly. | The car drops into the gap and, about a second later, stands before the ramp. |
| 12 | Watch the other car for a lap. | It follows the road, takes the ramp and comes round again without help. |
| 13 | Drive into the other car. | The cars push each other; neither passes through. |
| 14 | Right after `Go`, turn round, cross the line backwards, turn and cross it forwards. | The lap still reads `Lap 1/3`. |
| 15 | Complete a lap properly. | `Lap 2/3`. |
| 16 | Finish three laps. | `Finished`, a place that matches what happened, a total time, three lap times that add up to it. Enter leads to the title. |
| 17 | Count corners and cones on a lap. | At least four corners, both directions; at least six cones at the start. |

A game passes when all seventeen hold. A race a person cannot finish in ten tries fails
checks 15 and 16.
