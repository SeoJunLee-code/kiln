# Paddle duel

A written spec for one small 2D game. Build it from this text alone. Nobody will answer
questions.

## The game

1. A court seen from above: a wall along the top and the bottom, open at the left and
   right ends, a dashed line down the middle. A paddle stands near each end and a ball
   moves between them.
2. The left paddle moves up and down with W and S, the right one with the Up and Down
   arrows. Both can be moved at the same moment. A paddle cannot leave the court.
3. The ball bounces off the top and bottom walls and off the paddles. Where it meets a
   paddle decides the angle it leaves at: the middle sends it straight across, the ends
   send it away steeply, up from the upper end and down from the lower end, never
   steeper than 60 degrees from straight across.
4. Each time a paddle returns the ball, the ball gets 5% faster, up to twice the speed of
   the serve. The speed goes back to the serve's speed for the next point.
5. A ball that leaves by the left end is a point for the right player, and the other way
   round. The score of each side is shown at the top of its half.
6. After a point the ball waits in the middle for one second and is then served towards
   the player who lost the point, at an angle that is not always the same but is the
   same in two games played with the same key presses.
7. The first to 7 points wins. A panel shows `Left wins` or `Right wins` and
   `Press Enter`. Enter goes back to the menu.
8. The game opens on a menu with the game's name and three lines, one of which is marked:
   `Two players`, `One player`, `Watch`. Up and Down move the mark, Enter starts.
   - In `One player` the computer plays the right paddle.
   - In `Watch` the computer plays both.
9. The computer's paddle moves no faster than a player's. It returns most serves, and it
   can be beaten: a player who aims with the ends of the paddle wins points against it.
10. Escape during a game goes back to the menu and forgets the score.
11. A ball can never pass through a paddle or a wall, at any speed the game reaches.

## What "done" means

- Every file of the game is inside the folder `game/`.
- The game starts in the engine's ordinary way of running a game, with no error and no
  warning.
- Everything under "The game" holds for two people at one keyboard. Check as much of that
  as the engine lets you check without a person.

When done, list the files and say how the game is started.

## Screen pass checks

| # | Do | See |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Start the game. | A menu with a name and the three lines, one marked. No error or warning. |
| 2 | Press Down and Up. | The mark moves between the three lines. |
| 3 | Start `Two players`. | A court with two walls, a dashed middle line, two paddles, a ball, `0` and `0`. The ball waits about a second, then moves. |
| 4 | Hold W and Up together, then S and Down together. | Both paddles move at once, each way, and stop at the walls. |
| 5 | Let the ball hit the top or bottom wall. | It bounces. |
| 6 | Return the ball with the middle, the upper end and the lower end of a paddle. | Nearly straight; steeply up; steeply down. |
| 7 | Keep a rally going for ten returns. | The ball is clearly faster than at the serve. After the point, the next serve is slow again. |
| 8 | Let the ball leave on the left. | The right score reads 1. The ball waits in the middle and is served to the left. |
| 9 | Watch five serves. | Not all at the same angle. |
| 10 | Let one side reach 7. | `Left wins` or `Right wins` for that side, `Press Enter`; Enter shows the menu. |
| 11 | Start `One player` and touch nothing on the right. | The right paddle moves by itself, no faster than the left can, and returns most balls. |
| 12 | Play `One player` to the end, aiming with the paddle's ends. | The player can score; a person wins at least one game in five. |
| 13 | Start `Watch`. | Both paddles play; points are scored; a game ends with a winner. |
| 14 | Start `Watch` twice and note the score after one minute each time. | The same score. |
| 15 | Press Escape mid-game, then start again. | The menu; the new game starts 0 to 0. |
| 16 | During ten minutes of play over all modes. | The ball never passes through a paddle or a wall. |

A game passes when all sixteen hold.
