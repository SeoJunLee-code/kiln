# A rolling ball

A written spec for one small 3D game. Build it from this text alone. Nobody will answer
questions.

## The game

1. The game is in 3D. The player is a ball. The camera looks at the ball from behind and
   above and follows it. *(added)*
2. The arrow keys push the ball: up is away from the camera, down towards it, left and
   right to the sides. The ball rolls; it gains speed while a key is held, keeps rolling
   when the key is released, and slows down by itself over a few seconds. *(added: of the
   two control styles the source allows, this one)*
3. A level is a platform, or several joined ones, with empty space around and below. The
   edges are open unless this text says otherwise. Gravity pulls the ball down.
4. Every level has a starting area, where the ball begins, and a goal area that can be
   told apart from the rest of the floor. Touching the goal wins the level.
5. Every level holds at least five pickups. Rolling into a pickup collects it: it
   disappears and the score goes up by one.
6. The screen always shows three things during a level: the score, the lives left, and a
   timer that counts down from 60 seconds. *(numbers added)*
7. The player starts with three lives. A life is lost when the ball falls off the level
   or when the timer reaches zero. The level then starts again from its starting area
   with its pickups back and a full timer; the score keeps what it had when the level was
   entered. *(added: what a restart keeps)*
8. With no lives left, a screen shows `Game over`. Pressing Enter there goes back to the
   menu.
9. The game opens on a menu that shows the game's name and the words `Press Enter`.
   Pressing Enter starts level 1 with three lives and a score of zero.
10. There are three levels. *(added)*
    - **Level 1** is flat. The way from start to goal has at least one turn, so the goal
      cannot be reached by holding one key.
    - **Level 2** has a slope. The goal is higher than the start and is reached by rolling
      up a ramp. A ball left alone on the ramp rolls back down by itself.
    - **Level 3** has at least three loose boxes lying on the way to the goal. They are
      not walls: the ball moves them by rolling into them, they tumble and slide, and a
      box pushed over an edge falls away. The goal cannot be reached without moving at
      least one box.
11. Score and lives carry over from one level to the next. *(a stretch goal in the source)*
12. Touching the goal of level 3 shows an end screen with `You win` and the final score.

## What "done" means

- Every file of the game is inside the folder `game/`.
- The game starts in the engine's ordinary way of running a game, with no error and no
  warning.
- Everything under "The game" holds for a person who plays it with the arrow keys and
  Enter. Check as much of that as the engine lets you check without a person.

When done, list the files and say how the game is started.

## Screen pass checks

A person plays the game as the agent left it, with the arrow keys and Enter only, started
the way the agent's last message says. Each check is "do this, see that".

| # | Do | See |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Start the game. | A menu with a name and `Press Enter`. No error or warning where the game was started. |
| 2 | Press Enter. | Level 1 in 3D: a ball on a platform with empty space around it, seen from behind and above; a score of 0, 3 lives, a timer near 60 going down. |
| 3 | Hold up for one second, release. | The ball rolls away from the camera, visibly turning or sliding along; it keeps moving after the release and comes to rest within about ten seconds without a key. The camera follows. |
| 4 | Press each of the other three keys from rest. | The ball moves towards the camera, left and right. |
| 5 | Count pickups in level 1; roll into one. | At least five. The one touched disappears and the score reads 1. |
| 6 | Roll off an edge. | The ball falls. Lives read 2. The ball is back at the start, the pickup collected before is back, the timer is near 60 and the score is what it was when the level began. |
| 7 | Stay still until the timer reaches zero. | A life is lost and the level starts again in the same way. |
| 8 | Lose the last life. | `Game over`. Enter leads to the menu; Enter again starts level 1 with 3 lives and score 0. |
| 9 | Try to reach the goal of level 1 holding only one key from the start. | It cannot be done; reaching it takes a turn. Touching the goal starts level 2. |
| 10 | Enter level 2 with some score and fewer than 3 lives. | The score and lives are the ones level 1 ended with. |
| 11 | In level 2, roll halfway up the ramp and release every key. | The ball slows, stops and rolls back down. |
| 12 | Reach the goal of level 2. | It is higher than the start. Level 3 begins. |
| 13 | In level 3, roll into a box. | The box moves, and turns or slides; the ball is slowed by it. It is not fixed like a wall. |
| 14 | Push a box over an edge. | It falls away and does not come back until the level starts again. |
| 15 | Look for a way to the goal of level 3 that touches no box. | There is none. |
| 16 | Touch the goal of level 3. | `You win` and a score equal to the pickups collected in the three levels as played. |

A game passes when all sixteen hold. A level a person cannot win in ten tries fails its
check and the checks after it.
